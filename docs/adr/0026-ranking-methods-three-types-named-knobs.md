# Ranking Methods are three fixed types with named, explained settings — placement points, a rating, and a manual order — never a formula

Amended by [ADR 0030](./0030-team-rankings-chains-of-entries.md) (2026-10-15): the fourth knob family is the team family — the identity mode (Squad or shared players) with K and K', the contribution filter, and the weight a team's placement carries into its players' points (the subject, Players or teams, is no knob: it is fixed at the Ranking's creation, as the Method type is); a team Ranking's manual order ranks chains; in a team Discipline the entrants multiplier counts teams, and a Player Ranking's rating updates each lineup player against the opposing lineup's average; a Player exists for every Entrant with an identity, and a typed-name Entrant — the Organizer's, or a captain's on an anonymous-allowed Track — has none until claimed (ADR 0029); in a team Ranking typed names are linked per Entry's lineup, an Entry with fewer than K linked players opens an unlinked chain, and the refusal ships with the first team Ranking; on a team Ranking, a Season that starts from the previous Season's ratings matches each new chain, when it first appears, against the previous Season's final chains by the same predicate, each previous chain seeding at most one; and where field strength, head-to-head, or the automatic tiers read entrants' prior standing, a team Discipline's entrant is its Entry, given the row its credited lineup matches in a named team Revision and its credited players' average in a named Player Revision (ADR 0030).

[ADR 0025](./0025-rankings-are-a-publishers-reading-of-public-results.md) makes any Community a Ranking publisher. This decision fixes what a publisher can compute. The owner wanted publishers to tune as much as possible — how much a strong field is worth, how much beating someone counts — and asked whether the UltRank methodology (Smash's authoritative ranking: tournament tiers from entrants and the field's prior ranking values, player scores from wins and losses weighted by opponent strength and event value, attendance penalties, stated discretion) and start.gg's rankings and leagues (manual rankings with a free rating field; leagues with per-event tiers, per-tier placement points, public point adjustments with reasons, and attendance verification) had anything to teach. They did, and the shape below is their common ground: many settings, no free formulas, every number explainable per player. It mirrors ADR 0002 for formats (five families, no custom ones).

**Three Method types, fixed at the Ranking's creation.** A _placement-and-results_ Method scores each counted tournament's final standings (ADR 0005 derives them for every format; tied placements share a step and its points) and may fold in match results; a _rating_ Method updates one strength number per match in recorded order (Glicko-2 or a sibling; walkovers and disqualifications move nothing, draws count); a _manual_ Method is the publisher's own order over the Players who appear in the counted tournaments, each row carrying the results that back it and an optional free-form rating value (votes, an external Elo, an in-game rank), which is how a panel ranking like UltRank's is published here and what start.gg's rankings are. Equal scores or ratings share a rank ("tied third") unless the publisher picks a tie-break; which tie-breaks a type offers (head-to-head, best placement) is settled when that type is built. A Season can be seen under one Method only; a publisher who wants the same scope under another Method duplicates the Ranking with its scope and Seasons and changes the type. No type exposes a formula: the owner's reason is the one behind ADR 0002 — a formula cannot be validated, explained to the player it ranks, or compared across publishers.

**Settings are named knobs with defaults and presets, and the placement Method is where most of them live.** Each knob has a name, a one-line explanation, a default, and a range, and presets ("simple", "circuit", "full") are named bundles of values a publisher adjusts afterwards. The placement Method's knobs: the placement-points table, per tier where tiers are used; how a tournament's tier is assigned — automatically from entrants and field strength, or by the publisher per tournament; the entrants multiplier, whose default is smooth (rising by a fixed step per doubling of entrants, so 31 and 32 entrants are worth about the same) with stepped tiers only when chosen, because a threshold is where Organizers pad entry lists; _field strength_, how much the prior standing of the entrants raises a tournament's value, read from one Revision the publisher names — by default the previous Season's Revision at its end (its final standings, ADR 0025), or a named Revision of another Ranking's Season (UltRank reads regional rankings the same way); a later correction of that Season adds a Revision beside the named one and moves nothing here, and the Revision being computed is never a source, so a recomputation always gives the same answer; the named Revision's standings are copied into the Season, with the source's name and the Revision's instant, when the settings naming it are published or, if it is a Season's end still to come, when that Season ends, as seeding copies standings (ADR 0025), so deleting the source Ranking moves nothing in this Season; _head-to-head_, the share of a tournament's score that comes from whom a player beat and lost to, weighted by those opponents' prior standing, from zero (pure placement) upward, with repeat matchups counting less; best-N tournaments and attendance weighting; a minimum number of tournaments to be listed, 1 by default, and a minimum entrants per tournament (the placement and rating Methods share these, and the manual Method has the first, so its publisher places only those with enough counted tournaments; entrants are Participants with at least one non-walkover result); and point adjustments, which are public with a reason (ADR 0025). The rating Method's knobs are its initial value, volatility, inactivity handling, and whether a Season starts from the previous Season's ratings or from scratch. The first build ships the placement table, the entrants multiplier, best-N, the minimums, and adjustments; field strength, head-to-head, and automatic tiers come after; the rating Method after that. The manual Method is built first of all, since it exercises every part of ADR 0025 — Seasons, Revisions, Visibility, name linking, Player pages — with no computation.

**Typed names are linked by the publisher, inside their Ranking, and the person can refuse.** Rankings aggregate on Players (vision), and a Player exists for every self-made Entry; an Organizer-typed Entry has none until claimed. Rather than leave hand-run tournaments uncounted until every claim link is sent, a publisher may link a typed name to a Player within their own Ranking — the way every real ranking is assembled — and the link is one of the public interventions of ADR 0025, shown on the row it affects. It binds nothing outside that Ranking: not other Rankings, not the Player page. A Player sees the names linked to them across Rankings and may refuse one, which removes it and bars that publisher from re-linking it; a claim replaces a link with the real thing. A Player's acceptance of a link is the start of a claim — the tournament's Organizer confirms it in one step, keeping the claim's trust in the Organizer who knows the person — whose details the claim-verification decision owns. Discipline spellings work the same way: the operator's alias merge (vision) applies everywhere, and a publisher may treat spellings as one Discipline inside their Ranking meanwhile.

## Considered Options

- **A points table only.** It cannot express what the owner asked for (field strength, head-to-head) and cannot seed a bracket from few tournaments.
- **Free formulas** (a spreadsheet-like expression). The owner rejected them for the ADR 0002 reason; a wrong formula runs, and nobody can say why A is above B.
- **Re-implementing UltRank's algorithm** (iterated scores, volatility, outlier de-weighting, loss dragging). Bespoke to one discipline and opaque by its authors' own account; its mechanisms that generalize are the field-strength and head-to-head knobs, and a panel that computes it elsewhere publishes it through the manual Method's rating value.
- **Field strength from this Ranking's latest Revision.** Circular: which Revision is "previous" depends on when a correction is recomputed, so equal results could give unequal standings.
- **Name matching by string.** Breaks on the first "SF6" vs "Street Fighter 6" and on the first namesake.
- **Links with no refusal, or links that bind the Player page.** A third party's wrong public assertion about a person needs a remedy; a binding link would bypass the claim.
- **Settings on the Ranking rather than the Season.** A change for the new Season would recompute the finished ones.

## Consequences

- Per-player, per-tournament contributions are stored with each Revision, since a later correction changes what a recomputation would show for an old Revision; the breakdown is what "why am I third" displays.
- The intervention list of ADR 0025 includes the publisher's name links and tier assignments; a Player's refusals are stored on the Player.
- A Season stores a copy of the standings of each Revision it names, so its recomputations never read another Ranking.
- ADR 0005 gains a consumer that needs match order: results already carry their recording instant, which the rating Method uses as the sequence; corrections re-sequence by recomputation.
- [ADR 0030](./0030-team-rankings-chains-of-entries.md) (2026-10-15) adds the fourth knob family: identity mode and K, the contribution filter, and the players' weight (the subject is fixed at the Ranking's creation); it changes no type.
